1. What Safari Scout is

Safari Scout is a Safari extension and host app for macOS and iOS. It sends your questions and page content to an AI provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI), using your own API key. Safari Scout has no server of its own: nothing you do in Safari Scout is sent to the operator.

2. What we do not collect

Safari Scout contains no analytics, no crash telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and sets no cookies. The operator does not receive your key, page content, chat messages, or usage data.

4. Third parties

Your chosen AI provider receives the data in section 3 under your own account and its terms: Anthropic (Messages API) or OpenAI (Responses API). Read their privacy policies; Safari Scout cannot control provider handling. Apple distributes the app through the App Store. Provider processors and sub-processors: UNKNOWN until reviewed.

5. Sensitive data

Pages you view may contain personal, financial, or health information. Safari Scout redacts password and card fields before sending a page snapshot. It cannot recognise all sensitive text. Do not use Safari Scout on pages whose content you do not want sent to your provider.

6. Your choices

  • Settings: choose provider, model, history retention, per-site access.
  • Delete all data: removes both Keychain keys, extension storage, and

granted site access on this device.

  • Data held by your provider is deleted through your provider account.
