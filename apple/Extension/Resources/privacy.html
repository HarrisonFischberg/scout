1. What Scout for Safari is

Scout for Safari is a Safari extension and host app for macOS and iOS. It sends your questions and page content to an AI provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI), using your own API key. Scout for Safari has no server of its own: nothing you do in Scout for Safari is sent to the operator.

2. What we do not collect

Scout for Safari contains no analytics, no crash telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and sets no cookies. The operator does not receive your key, page content, chat messages, or usage data.

4. Third parties

Your chosen AI provider receives the data in section 3 under your own account and its terms: Anthropic (Messages API) or OpenAI (Responses API). Scout for Safari asks for your permission once per provider before it sends anything to that provider, names the provider and what is sent, and links to its privacy policy and to the terms for its API. Nothing is sent if you choose Not now. The providers' privacy policies say they do not cover content sent through their business offerings such as the API; requests made with your own API key fall under Anthropic's Commercial Terms or OpenAI's API data commitments. Read their privacy policies; Scout for Safari cannot control provider handling. Apple distributes the app through the App Store. Provider processors and sub-processors: UNKNOWN until reviewed.

5. Sensitive data

Pages you view may contain personal, financial, or health information. Scout for Safari redacts password and card fields before sending a page snapshot. It cannot recognise all sensitive text. Do not use Scout for Safari on pages whose content you do not want sent to your provider.

6. Your choices

  • Settings: choose provider, model, history retention, per-site access.
  • Sharing with AI providers: see which provider you allowed, and withdraw it.

After you withdraw, nothing is sent to that provider until you allow it again.

  • Delete all data: removes both Keychain keys, extension storage, and

granted site access on this device.

  • Data held by your provider is deleted through your provider account.
